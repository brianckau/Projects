# Claim verification and adversarial review

## Trace the decision

- Record each decision-critical claim as `claim ID | statement | type | evidence IDs | assumption IDs | calculation | contradiction | status | owner`.
- Label the claim as reported fact, issuer statement, provider estimate, inference or scenario assumption. Mark supplied inputs, retrieved evidence and derived values separately. Give the source date, measurement period, document locator and units needed to reproduce the claim.
- Keep the source/evidence ledger as specified in research-and-agents.md. Inspect original passages for decisive claims. Flag mismatched periods, comparability, stale or superseded disclosures, selective sourcing and missing coverage. A shared upstream source counts once.
- For valuation, record the price date, benchmark provenance, material assumptions and ranges. Show what would already have to be priced in to support the thesis. If the market baseline or value bridge is missing, keep the edge provisional and identify the data needed.
- Record corrections as `original claim → challenge and evidence → revised claim → effect on judgment`. Preserve contradictory evidence and uncertainty. Missing causal evidence can leave a claim unresolved even when an arithmetic check passes.

## Try to disprove the leading candidate

- Before returning the highest-ranked candidate, give the existing independent bull-bear mapper or a dedicated reviewer the synthesized candidate, claim IDs and original source locators. Withhold the coordinator's preferred verdict. Reuse worker capacity and the shared research budget.
- Ask the reviewer to test the weakest dependency, retrieve or inspect the strongest contrary evidence where available, reproduce one decisive calculation, and compare the strongest plausible competing explanation. Check whether the product advantage matters to the buyer, whether the economics scale, and whether the market already expects it.
- Require a verdict of supported, provisional or contradicted, with the reason, surviving risk and one observable falsifier. Have the coordinator resolve material errors and downgrade or discard candidates that fail. If no candidate survives, report that result. When independent agents are unavailable, label the adversarial pass as a sequential self-check.
- Show the decisive correction or remaining gap in the concise snapshot. Leave a human analyst to accept assumptions, choose the next evidence check, and decide whether to act. Record human review as pending unless an analyst explicitly confirms it.
